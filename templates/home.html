What is Loqelo? +

Loqelo is a platform being built to connect customers with service providers for home, garden and office projects.

When will Loqelo launch? +

The planned launch is February 2027. Join the waitlist to hear about launch updates.

Who can join the waitlist? +

Customers looking for help and service providers offering their skills can both register. Choose the appropriate option in the form.

Does joining cost anything? +

Joining this pre-launch waitlist is free. Any future platform pricing will be communicated by Loqelo.

How will my details be used? +

Your contact details are collected to manage the waitlist and, if you consent, send Loqelo launch updates. Please review the final privacy policy before the website goes live.
